If i want to change what type of login credential 
will be accepted, go to appsettings.json and change
the login type to true/false;

"LoginSettings": {
      "AllowEmailLogin": false,
      "AllowUsernameLogin": false,
      "AllowStaffIdLogin": true,
      "PrimaryLoginMethod": "StaffId"
    },

Then go to C:\ATMS_New\ATMS.API\Helpers\LoginSettings.cs
and also update it
